Course index · MonoGame RT4X

Building a fantasy real-time 4X in MonoGame

This course turns a blank MonoGame project into the foundations of a lo-fi, top-down fantasy real-time 4X game — the monogame-rt4x subject repository. Each lesson is short, hands-on, and ends in one tangible win you build on. You write the code in the subject repo; these lessons and references stay here to guide and remind you.

Work through the lessons in order — each assumes the one before it. The references are the compressed, printable essence of the lessons; return to them whenever you need a quick reminder. Ask me follow-up questions at any point: I'm your teacher, not just a page of notes.

Lessons

Foundations · get it building and running

  • Lesson 1 — Verify the game loop 15 min Create and run a MonoGame window, and separate Update (simulation) from Draw (presentation).
  • Lesson 2 — Convert to a Content Builder project 20 min Replace the legacy Content.mgcb pipeline with a reviewed, C#-based Content Builder project before any assets arrive.

Rendering · the first thing you see

  • Lesson 3 — Draw a screen-space grid 20 min Draw a top-down playfield entirely from code, turning a single 1×1 pixel into every grid line.

Interaction · point at the world

  • Lesson 4 — Highlight the hovered cell 20 min Map the mouse to a grid cell with quick inline math and highlight it — deliberately the "before" state for the refactor to come.

Coordinate systems · theory then refactor

  • Lesson 5 — Understand coordinate systems 25 min The knowledge lesson: screen, world, and grid spaces, transform matrices, the conversion chain, and the traps that cause off-by-one clicks.
  • Lesson 6 — Build coordinate utilities and camera 40 min Lift the inline math from Lesson 4 into a testable Transforms utility, a Camera that owns the view matrix, and convenience methods on Grid.
  • Coming soon — Camera controls Planned Map WASD/arrows to pan and the mouse wheel to zoom, and confirm cell picking still lands correctly while the view moves.

The mission

The goal is to grow monogame-rt4x from a working prototype into an independently maintainable fantasy RT4X — real-time play with time controls, empire building, and lo-fi visuals — while learning practical MonoGame along the way. Lessons stay tied to that mission so nothing feels like an abstract tutorial exercise.

Built with the teach approach. Lessons live in docs/lessons/, references in docs/reference/. Ask me anything that's unclear — that's what I'm here for.
